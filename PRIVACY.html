Privacy Policy

How Klunk - Log Viewer handles your data.

Last Updated: November 20, 2024

Klunk - Log Viewer is a local desktop application. It does not collect, transmit, or store any of your data on external servers. Everything stays on your machine.

1. Data Processing and Storage

All primary data processing occurs on your local machine.

  • Log Data: When you open log files (local or remote), the application parses them and may store them in a temporary local SQLite database to enable high-performance viewing and filtering. This data remains on your local machine and is never transmitted to the developers or any third party.
  • Remote Connections: If you connect to external sources (SFTP servers, Kubernetes clusters, external databases, or URLs), the application transmits only the credentials and requests you provide. These connections are initiated solely by your actions and go directly to the target system — not through any developer-controlled infrastructure.
  • Configuration: Your settings, Most Recently Used (MRU) items, saved patterns, and bookmarks are stored locally in the application's data directory on your device.

2. No Data Collection or Telemetry

  • Klunk - Log Viewer does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, usage data, or telemetry to the developers or any third parties.
  • The application does not use cookies, tracking pixels, analytics SDKs, or any other tracking technology.
  • No crash reports, diagnostics, or feature-usage metrics are sent anywhere.
Your log data never leaves your machine unless you connect it to an external source.

3. Third-Party Services

The application allows you to connect to third-party services such as your own SFTP servers, Kubernetes clusters, or databases. Your use of those services is governed entirely by their respective privacy policies and terms of use. Klunk - Log Viewer has no visibility into or control over those systems.

4. Credential Security

Passwords and SSH key passphrases you enter for remote connections are encrypted before being stored in the local configuration. We recommend:

  • Using standard OS-level security (disk encryption, screen lock) to protect your device.
  • Using SSH key authentication rather than passwords where possible.
  • Not sharing your application data directory with untrusted users.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the Last Updated date at the top of this document and in the application's release notes.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please open an issue in the project repository.
